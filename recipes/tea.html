Tea

Description

A simple and refreshing cup of tea that can be enjoyed any time of the day. Easy to prepare and perfect for relaxing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tea bag
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • Milk (optional)

Steps

  1. Step : Pour water into a kettle or saucepan.
  2. Step : Bring the water to a boil.
  3. Step : Place the tea bag in a cup.
  4. Step : Pour the hot water over the tea bag.
  5. Step : Let the tea steep for 3 to 5 minutes.
  6. Step: Remove the tea bag and add sugar or milk if desired.
  7. Step : Stir well and enjoy.
