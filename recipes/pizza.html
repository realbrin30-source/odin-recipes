Pizza

Description

A homemade pizza recipe with a crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, and melted mozzarella cheese. Perfect for family dinners and easy to customize with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Pepperoni slices (optional)
  • Fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Step : Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Step : Roll out the pizza dough and place it on a pizza pan or baking sheet.
  3. Step : Brush the dough lightly with olive oil.
  4. Step : Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Step : Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce and add desired toppings.
  6. Step : Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden brown.
  7. Step : Remove from the oven, garnish with basil, slice, and serve.
